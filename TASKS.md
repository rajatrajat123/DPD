## Permanent Product & Engineering Mandate: Media Optimization, Moderation & Delivery

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Mandatory Media Requirement (Permanent Invariant Across All Phases):**  
> Whenever any user uploads an image or video (profile photos, verification selfies, chat attachments, or editorial assets):
> 1. **Never Serve Raw Uploads Publicly:** Raw user uploads are ingested into a quarantined private object storage prefix (`s3://aura-media-quarantine`) and are never exposed via public URLs.
> 2. **Strict Upload Validation:** Verify magic bytes (not just file extensions), enforce size/duration caps (`15 MiB` max image, `100 MiB` / `60s` max video), and reject unsupported or polyglot containers.
> 3. **Mandatory EXIF & GPS Metadata Stripping:** Strip all EXIF, IPTC, XMP, and embedded GPS coordinates before persisting any derivative to protect LGBTQ+ user safety and location privacy.
> 4. **Automated Image Resizing, Compression & Next-Gen Formats:** Generate multi-resolution derivatives (`thumb` 150×150, `card` 600×800, `full` 1080×1440) in both **AVIF** and **WebP** (with progressive JPEG fallback), plus an inline BlurHash/LQIP placeholder.
> 5. **Automated Video Transcoding:** Transcode uploaded videos via FFmpeg into adaptive **HLS** (`360p`, `720p`, `1080p`) and optimized MP4 (`H.264/AAC` with `faststart`), plus an extracted WebP/AVIF poster frame.
> 6. **Automated CSAM & NSFW Moderation Gate:** Scan every asset for CSAM (PhotoDNA/hash matching) and policy-violating NSFW content before transitioning `moderation_status` to `APPROVED`.
> 7. **Non-Blocking Durable Worker Jobs:** All media transcoding and moderation runs asynchronously via durable jobs triggered by the PostgreSQL transactional outbox (`media.asset.uploaded.v1`) with exponential backoff and dead-letter handling.
> 8. **Signed CDN Delivery & Lifecycle Cleanup:** Approved derivatives are stored in private `s3://aura-media-approved` and served exclusively via short-lived signed CloudFront/CDN URLs (`Cache-Control: private, max-age=3600`). Rejected, replaced, or deleted assets and all derivatives are purged within 24 hours.
